qspec.models. hf_trans  (  i ,  j_l ,  j_u  )[source]

All allowed electric dipole transitions $F_l\rightarrow F_u$ between two hyperfine-structure multiplets and their hyperfine coefficients, see hf_coeff.

Parameters:
iscalar

The nuclear spin quantum number $I$.

j_lscalar

The electronic total angular momentum quantum number $J_l$ of the lower state.

j_uscalar

The electronic total angular momentum quantum number $J_u$ of the upper state.

Returns:
transitionslist[list[tuple]]

A list of transitions [(f_l, f_u), coeffs_l, coeffs_u].
