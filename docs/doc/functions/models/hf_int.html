qspec.models. hf_int  (  i ,  j_l ,  j_u ,  transitions  )[source]

The relative line intensities (Racah intensities) of hyperfine transitions $F_l\rightarrow F_u$

$$ I_{F_lF_u} = (2F_l + 1)(2F_u + 1)\begin{Bmatrix}J_l & F_l & I\\ F_u & J_u & 1\end{Bmatrix}^2. $$
Parameters:
iscalar

The nuclear spin quantum number $I$.

j_lscalar

The electronic total angular momentum quantum number $J_l$ of the lower state.

j_uscalar

The electronic total angular momentum quantum number $J_u$ of the upper state.

transitionsIterable

A list of transitions, see hf_trans. The first element of each entry must be the pair (f_l, f_u).

Returns:
intensitieslist[ndarray]

The relative line intensities $I_{F_lF_u}$.
