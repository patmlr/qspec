qspec.models. fwhm_voigt_d  (  gamma ,  gamma_d ,  sigma ,  sigma_d  )[source]

The uncertainty of the full width at half maximum (FWHM) of a Voigt profile, see fwhm_voigt

$$ \Delta\delta\nu = \sqrt{\left(\frac{8\ln(2)\sigma\Delta\sigma}{\sqrt{0.2166\Gamma^2 + 8\ln(2)\sigma^2}}\right)^2 + \left(\left(0.5346 + \frac{0.2166\Gamma}{\sqrt{0.2166\Gamma^2 + 8\ln(2)\sigma^2}}\right)\Delta\Gamma\right)^2}. $$
Parameters:
gammaarray_like

The FWHM $\Gamma$ of the Lorentzian (arb. units).

gamma_darray_like

The uncertainty $\Delta\Gamma$ of gamma (unit of gamma).

sigmaarray_like

The standard deviation $\sigma$ of the Gaussian (unit of gamma).

sigma_darray_like

The uncertainty $\Delta\sigma$ of sigma (unit of gamma).

Returns:
fwhm_dndarray

The uncertainty $\Delta\delta\nu$ of the FWHM of the Voigt profile (unit of gamma).
