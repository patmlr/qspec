qspec.models. get_all_f  (  i ,  j  )[source]

All total angular momentum quantum numbers $F$ of one or several fine-structure states.

Parameters:
iscalar

The nuclear spin(s) $I$.

jscalar

The electronic total angular momentum quantum number(s) $J$.

Returns:
F_listlist[quant]

The sorted list of all possible $F$ quantum numbers of all combinations of i and j.
