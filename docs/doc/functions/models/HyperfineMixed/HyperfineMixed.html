qspec.models. HyperfineMixed  (  model ,  i ,  j_l ,  j_u ,  label=None ,  config=None  )[source]

A hyperfine structure model including the hyperfine-induced mixing of fine-structure states with the same $F$, based on [ W. R. Johnson et al., Phys. Rev. A 55, 2728 (1997)]. For the mixed lower or upper states, the peak positions are the eigenvalues of Eq. (2.5) of the reference, parameterized by the fine-structure energies FS_l{i}(J) and FS_u{i}(J). For states without mixing, the hyperfine structure constants A_l{i}(J), B_l{i}(J), ... are used as in Hyperfine. The intensities are named int{i}(...).

Parameters:
modelModel

A submodel whose parameters are adopted by this model.

iscalar

The nuclear spin quantum number $I$.

j_lscalar

The electronic total angular momentum quantum number $J_l$ of the lower state.

j_uscalar

The electronic total angular momentum quantum number $J_u$ of the upper state.

labelstr | None

A label for the Splitter (isotope / isomer / transition).

configdict | None

The configuration of the hyperfine-induced mixing. This is a dictionary such as {"enabled_l": False, "enabled_u": False, "Jl": [0.5], "Ju": [0.5], "Tl": [[1.0]], "Tu": [[1.0]], "fl": [0.0], "fu": [0.0], "mu": 0.0}, where

  • "enabled_l"/"enabled_u" decide if the corresponding lower/upper states mix,
  • "Jl"/"Ju" are lists of the lower/upper state $J$, including j_l and j_u,
  • "Tl"/"Tu" are the matrix representations of the electronic magnetic dipole operator, see the reference,
  • "fl"/"fu" are lists of the initial fine-structure energies (can be omitted) and
  • "mu" is the magnetic dipole moment of the nucleus ($\mu_\mathrm{N}$).

If None, the mixing is disabled.
