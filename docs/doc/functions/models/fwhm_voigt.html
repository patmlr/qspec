qspec.models. fwhm_voigt  (  gamma ,  sigma  )[source]

The full width at half maximum (FWHM) of a Voigt profile

$$ \delta\nu = 0.5346\Gamma + \sqrt{0.2166\Gamma^2 + 8\ln(2)\sigma^2}. $$
Parameters:
gammaarray_like

The FWHM $\Gamma$ of the Lorentzian (arb. units).

sigmaarray_like

The standard deviation $\sigma$ of the Gaussian (unit of gamma).

Returns:
fwhmndarray

The FWHM $\delta\nu$ of the Voigt profile (unit of gamma).
