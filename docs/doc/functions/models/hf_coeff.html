qspec.models. hf_coeff  (  i ,  j ,  f  )[source]

The coefficients of the hyperfine structure constants $A$, $B$ and $C$ of the magnetic dipole, electric quadrupole and magnetic octupole order, such that the hyperfine structure shift of the state is $\Delta_\mathrm{hfs} = c_AA + c_BB + c_CC$, see qspec.physics.hyperfine. Only the orders allowed by $I$ and $J$ are returned.

Parameters:
iscalar

The nuclear spin quantum number $I$.

jscalar

The electronic total angular momentum quantum number $J$.

fscalar

The total angular momentum quantum number $F$.

Returns:
coeffstuple[float, ...]

The tuple of hyperfine coefficients $(c_A, c_B, c_C)$ of the quantum state.
