qspec. mode_lognormal  (  x ,  bins=100  )[source]

Estimate the mode of a sample by fitting a lognormal distribution to its histogram. The histogram and the fitted distribution are plotted.

Parameters:
xarray_like

The sample data.

binsarray_like

The number of bins or the bin edges of the histogram, see numpy.histogram.

Returns:
modefloat

The mode of the lognormal distribution.
