qspec. a  (  i ,  j_l ,  f_l ,  j_u ,  f_u ,  as_sympy=False  )[source]

The coefficient $A_F^{F^\prime}$, parameterizing the isotropic part of the perturbative differential scattering rate

$$ A_F^{F^\prime} = \frac{(2F_l + 1)(2F_u + 1)(2J_u + 1)}{3(2J_l + 1)(2I + 1)} \begin{Bmatrix} J_u & J_l & 1 \\ F_l & F_u & I \end{Bmatrix}^2, $$

as described in [R. C. Brown et al., Phys. Rev. A 87, 032504 (2013)].

Parameters:
isympy_scalar

The nuclear spin quantum number $I$.

j_lsympy_scalar

The electronic total angular momentum quantum number $J_l$ of the lower state.

f_lsympy_scalar

The total angular momentum quantum number $F_l$ of the lower state.

j_usympy_scalar

The electronic total angular momentum quantum number $J_u$ of the upper state.

f_usympy_scalar

The total angular momentum quantum number $F_u$ of the upper state.

as_sympybool

Whether to return the result as a sympy expression (True) or as a float (False).

Returns:
Asympy_expr | float

The $A_F^{F^\prime}$ coefficient.
