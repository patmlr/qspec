--- layout: api title: qspec.c_dipole ---
qspec. c_dipole  (  i ,  j_i ,  f_i ,  m_i ,  j_u ,  f_u ,  j_f ,  f_f ,  m_f ,  theta_l ,  scatter_pol ,  as_sympy=False  )[source]

The transition dipole element for the scattering $|i\rangle\rightarrow|F^\prime m^\prime\rangle\rightarrow|f\rangle$ of linearly polarized light into the polarization $\epsilon$

$$\begin{aligned} C_{i\rightarrow f, x}^{F^\prime} &= -\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\sum\limits_{m^\prime} \left[(A_f)_1 - (A_f)_{-1}\right]a_\mathrm{L}\\[1ex] C_{i\rightarrow f, y}^{F^\prime} &= \frac{\mathrm{i}}{\sqrt{2}}\sum\limits_{m^\prime} \left[(A_f)_1 + (A_f)_{-1}\right]a_\mathrm{L}\\[1ex] a_\mathrm{L} &= \cos(\theta_\mathrm{L})(A_i)_0 - \frac{\mathrm{i}}{\sqrt{2}}\sin(\theta_\mathrm{L})\left[(A_i)_1 + (A_i)_{-1}\right], \end{aligned}$$

with the spherical transition dipole elements $(A_i)_q = (A_{F_im_i}^{F^\prime m^\prime})_q$ and $(A_f)_q = (A_{F_fm_f}^{F^\prime m^\prime})_q$ a_dipole, as described in [R. C. Brown et al., Phys. Rev. A 87, 032504 (2013)].

Parameters:
isympy_scalar

The nuclear spin quantum number $I$.

j_isympy_scalar

The electronic total angular momentum quantum number $J_i$ of the initial lower state.

f_isympy_scalar

The total angular momentum quantum number $F_i$ of the initial lower state.

m_isympy_scalar

The magnetic quantum number $m_i$ of the total angular momentum of the initial lower state.

j_usympy_scalar

The electronic total angular momentum quantum number $J_u$ of the intermediate upper state.

f_usympy_scalar

The total angular momentum quantum number $F_u$ of the intermediate upper state.

j_fsympy_scalar

The electronic total angular momentum quantum number $J_f$ of the final lower state.

f_fsympy_scalar

The total angular momentum quantum number $F_f$ of the final lower state.

m_fsympy_scalar

The magnetic quantum number $m_f$ of the total angular momentum of the final lower state.

theta_lsympy_scalar

The angle $\theta_\mathrm{L}$ between the electric field of the linearly polarized incoming photon and the direction of detection.

scatter_polstr

The polarization $\epsilon$ of the scattered light. Can be either "x" or "y".

as_sympybool

Whether to return the result as a sympy expression (True) or as a float (False).

Returns:
c_dipolesympy_expr | float

The transition dipole element $C_{i\rightarrow f}^{F^\prime}$.
