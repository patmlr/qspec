qspec. clebsch_gordan  (  j1 ,  j2 ,  j3 ,  m1 ,  m2 ,  m3 ,  as_sympy=False  )[source]

The Clebsch-Gordan coefficient

$$ C_{J_1 m_1, J_2 m_2}^{J_3 m_3} \coloneqq \langle J_1, m_1, J_2, m_2\, |\, J_3, m_3\rangle. $$
Parameters:
j1sympy_scalar

The angular momentum quantum number $J_1$.

j2sympy_scalar

The angular momentum quantum number $J_2$.

j3sympy_scalar

The angular momentum quantum number $J_3$.

m1sympy_scalar

The magnetic quantum number $m_1$.

m2sympy_scalar

The magnetic quantum number $m_2$.

m3sympy_scalar

The magnetic quantum number $m_3$.

as_sympybool

Whether to return the result as a sympy expression (True) or as a float (False).

Returns:
cgsympy_expr | float

The Clebsch-Gordan coefficient $C_{J_1 m_1, J_2 m_2}^{J_3 m_3}$.
