--- layout: api title: qspec.a_dipole ---
qspec. a_dipole  (  i ,  j_l ,  f_l ,  m_l ,  j_u ,  f_u ,  m_u ,  q ,  as_sympy=False  )[source]

The spherical transition dipole element

$$ (A_{Fm}^{F^\prime m^\prime})_q = (-1)^{F_l + I + 1 + J_u}\sqrt{(2F_l + 1)(2J_u + 1)} \begin{Bmatrix} J_u & J_l & 1 \\ F_l & F_u & I \end{Bmatrix}\langle F_l, m_l, 1, q\, |\, F_u, m_u\rangle, $$

which is zero unless $m_u - m_l = q$, as described in [R. C. Brown et al., Phys. Rev. A 87, 032504 (2013)].

Parameters:
isympy_scalar

The nuclear spin quantum number $I$.

j_lsympy_scalar

The electronic total angular momentum quantum number $J_l$ of the lower state.

f_lsympy_scalar

The total angular momentum quantum number $F_l$ of the lower state.

m_lsympy_scalar

The magnetic quantum number $m_l$ of the total angular momentum of the lower state.

j_usympy_scalar

The electronic total angular momentum quantum number $J_u$ of the upper state.

f_usympy_scalar

The total angular momentum quantum number $F_u$ of the upper state.

m_usympy_scalar

The magnetic quantum number $m_u$ of the total angular momentum of the upper state.

qsympy_scalar

The polarization component $q$. Must be in {-1, 0, 1} for $\sigma^-$, $\pi$ and $\sigma^+$ light.

as_sympybool

Whether to return the result as a sympy expression (True) or as a float (False).

Returns:
a_dipolesympy_expr | float

The spherical transition dipole element $(A_{Fm}^{F^\prime m^\prime})_q$.
