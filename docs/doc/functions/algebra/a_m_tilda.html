--- layout: api title: qspec.a_m_tilda ---
qspec. a_m_tilda  (  i ,  j_l ,  f_l ,  m_l ,  j_u ,  f_u ,  m_u ,  as_sympy=False  )[source]

The relative peak strength in a hyperfine structure Zeeman spectrum

$$ f_{Fm}^{F^\prime m^\prime} = (2F_l + 1)(2F_u + 1)\langle F_l, m_l, 1, m_u - m_l\, |\, F_u, m_u\rangle^2 \begin{Bmatrix} J_u & J_l & 1 \\ F_l & F_u & I \end{Bmatrix}^2, $$

as used in TILDA.

Parameters:
isympy_scalar

The nuclear spin quantum number $I$.

j_lsympy_scalar

The electronic total angular momentum quantum number $J_l$ of the lower state.

f_lsympy_scalar

The total angular momentum quantum number $F_l$ of the lower state.

m_lsympy_scalar

The magnetic quantum number $m_l$ of the total angular momentum of the lower state.

j_usympy_scalar

The electronic total angular momentum quantum number $J_u$ of the upper state.

f_usympy_scalar

The total angular momentum quantum number $F_u$ of the upper state.

m_usympy_scalar

The magnetic quantum number $m_u$ of the total angular momentum of the upper state.

as_sympybool

Whether to return the result as a sympy expression (True) or as a float (False).

Returns:
fsympy_expr | float

The relative peak strength $f_{Fm}^{F^\prime m^\prime}$.
