--- layout: api title: qspec.mu_j_m1 ---
qspec. mu_j_m1  (  s ,  l ,  j_l ,  j_u ,  as_sympy=False  )[source]

The reduced magnetic dipole transition matrix element

$$\mu_{J_l}^{J_u}\coloneqq\langle LS,J_l||\vec{\mu}||LS,J_u\rangle$$

in units of the Bohr magneton $\mu_\mathrm{B}$.

Parameters:
ssympy_scalar

The electronic spin quantum number $S$ of the lower and upper state.

lsympy_scalar

The electronic orbital angular momentum quantum number $L$ of the lower and upper state.

j_lsympy_scalar

The electronic total angular momentum quantum number $J_l$ of the lower state.

j_usympy_scalar

The electronic total angular momentum quantum number $J_u$ of the upper state.

as_sympybool

Return the result as a symbol (True) or as a float (False).

Returns:
outsympy_expr | float

The magnetic dipole transition matrix element $\mu_{J_l}^{J_u}$ ($\mu_\mathrm{B}$).
