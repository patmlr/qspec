qspec. b  (  i ,  j_l ,  f_l ,  j_u ,  f_u ,  as_sympy=False  )[source]

The coefficient $B_F^{F^\prime}$, parameterizing the classical angle-dependent part of the perturbative differential scattering rate

$$ B_F^{F^\prime} = \frac{2}{3}\left[f(0) - f(\pi / 2)\right], $$

with the $f$ function f_0, as described in [R. C. Brown et al., Phys. Rev. A 87, 032504 (2013)].

Parameters:
isympy_scalar

The nuclear spin quantum number $I$.

j_lsympy_scalar

The electronic total angular momentum quantum number $J_l$ of the lower state.

f_lsympy_scalar

The total angular momentum quantum number $F_l$ of the lower state.

j_usympy_scalar

The electronic total angular momentum quantum number $J_u$ of the upper state.

f_usympy_scalar

The total angular momentum quantum number $F_u$ of the upper state.

as_sympybool

Whether to return the result as a sympy expression (True) or as a float (False).

Returns:
Bsympy_expr | float

The $B_F^{F^\prime}$ coefficient.
