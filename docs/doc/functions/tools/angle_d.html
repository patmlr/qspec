The uncertainty of the angle between two vectors, see angle.

Parameters:
xarray_like

The first vectors (arb. units).

x_darray_like

The uncertainties of the first vectors (unit of x).

yarray_like

The second vectors (arb. units).

y_darray_like

The uncertainties of the second vectors (unit of y).

axisint

The axis along which the vector components are aligned.

Returns:
