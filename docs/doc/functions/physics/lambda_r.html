qspec.lambda_r  (  r ,  r_d ,  r_ref ,  r_ref_d ,  delta_r ,  delta_r_d ,  v2 ,  v2_ref ,  v4 ,  v4_ref ,  v6 ,  v6_ref ,  c2c1 ,  c3c1  )[source]

Parameters:
rarray_like

The Barrett radius of an isotope.

r_darray_like

The uncertainty of the Barrett radius.

r_refarray_like

The Barrett radius of a reference isotope.

r_ref_darray_like

The uncertainty of the Barrett radius of the reference isotope.

delta_rarray_like

The difference between the Barrett radius of the isotope and the reference isotope.

delta_r_darray_like

The uncertainty of the difference between the Barrett radius of the isotope and the reference isotope.

v2array_like

The V2 factor of the isotope.

v2_refarray_like

The V2 factor of the reference isotope.

v4array_like

The V4 factor of the isotope.

v4_refarray_like

The V4 factor of the reference isotope.

v6array_like

The V6 factor of the isotope.

v6_refarray_like

The V6 factor of the reference isotope.

c2c1array_like

Seltzer's coefficient for the quartic moment.

c3c1array_like

Seltzer's coefficient for the sextic moment.

Returns:
outNone

The difference of the mean sextic nuclear charge radius between two isotopes and its uncertainty.
