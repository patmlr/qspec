qspec. freq_to_wavelength  (  f  )[source]

Convert a frequency in MHz into a wavelength in μm

$$ \lambda = \frac{c}{f}. $$
Parameters:
farray_like

The frequency $f$ of a transition (MHz).

Returns:
lambdandarray

The wavelength $\lambda$ corresponding to the frequency f (μm).
