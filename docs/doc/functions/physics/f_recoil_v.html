qspec. f_recoil_v  (  v ,  alpha ,  f_lab ,  m  )[source]

The change of a transition frequency of an atom moving with velocity $v$ due to the absorption of a laser photon with frequency $f_\mathrm{lab}$ in the laboratory frame

$$ \delta f = \frac{hf^{\prime 2}}{2mc^2}, $$

where $f^\prime$ is the Doppler-shifted frequency in the rest frame of the atom, see doppler.

Parameters:
varray_like

The velocity $v$ of the atom (m/s).

alphaarray_like

The angle $\alpha$ between a velocity- and a wave-vector in the laboratory frame (rad).

f_labarray_like

The frequency of light $f_\mathrm{lab}$ in the laboratory frame (MHz).

marray_like

The mass $m$ of the atom (u).

Returns:
df_recndarray

The change of the transition frequency $\delta f$ (MHz).
