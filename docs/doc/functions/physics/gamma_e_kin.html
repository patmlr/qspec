The time-dilation/Lorentz factor

$$ \gamma = 1 + \frac{E_\mathrm{kin}}{mc^2}. $$
Parameters:

The kinetic energy $E_\mathrm{kin}$ of a body (eV).

marray_like

The mass $m$ of the body (u).

Returns:
gammandarray

The time-dilation/Lorentz factor $\gamma$ corresponding to the kinetic energy e of a body with mass m.
