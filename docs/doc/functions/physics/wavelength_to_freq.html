qspec. wavelength_to_freq  (  lam  )[source]

Convert a wavelength in μm into a frequency in MHz

$$ f = \frac{c}{\lambda}. $$
Parameters:
lamarray_like

The wavelength $\lambda$ of a transition (μm).

Returns:
freqndarray

The frequency $f$ corresponding to the wavelength lam (MHz).
