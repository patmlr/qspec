qspec. wavelength_to_inv_cm  (  lam  )[source]

Convert a wavelength in μm into a wavenumber in cm-1

$$ k = \frac{10^4}{\lambda}. $$
Parameters:
lamarray_like

The wavelength $\lambda$ of a transition (μm).

Returns:
kndarray

The wavenumber $k$ corresponding to the wavelength lam (cm-1).
