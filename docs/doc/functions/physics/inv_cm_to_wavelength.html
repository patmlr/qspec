qspec. inv_cm_to_wavelength  (  k  )[source]

Convert a wavenumber in cm-1 into a wavelength in μm

$$ \lambda = \frac{10^4}{k}. $$
Parameters:
karray_like

The wavenumber $k$ of a transition (cm-1).

Returns:
lambdandarray

The wavelength $\lambda$ corresponding to the wavenumber k (μm).
