qspec. get_m  (  f  )[source]

All magnetic quantum numbers of the total angular momentum quantum number $F$

$$ m \in \{-F, -F + 1, \dots, F\}. $$
Parameters:
fscalar

The total angular momentum quantum number $F$.

Returns:
m_listlist[quant]

All possible magnetic quantum numbers $m$ of the specified quantum number $F$.
