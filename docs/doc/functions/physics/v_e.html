qspec.v_e  (  e ,  m ,  v0 = 0 ,  relativistic = True  )[source]

Parameters:
enumpy.ndarray | Iterable | int | float

Energy which is added to the kinetic energy of a body with velocity v0 (eV).

mnumpy.ndarray | Iterable | int | float

The mass of the body (amu).

v0numpy.ndarray | Iterable | int | float

The initial velocity of the body (m/s).

relativisticNone

The calculation is performed either relativistically or classically.

Returns:
outnumpy.ndarray | Iterable | int | float

The velocity of a body with mass m and velocity v0 after the addition of the kinetic energy e (m/s).
