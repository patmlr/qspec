qspec. get_f  (  i ,  j  )[source]

All total angular momentum quantum numbers of the coupled angular momenta $\vec{F} = \vec{I} + \vec{J}$

$$ F \in \{|I - J|, |I - J| + 1, \dots, I + J\}. $$
Parameters:
iscalar

The nuclear spin quantum number $I$.

jscalar

The electronic total angular momentum quantum number $J$.

Returns:
F_listlist[quant]

All possible $F$ quantum numbers.
