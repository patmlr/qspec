qspec. xi_t  (  t ,  f ,  u ,  q ,  m  )[source]

The asymmetry parameter of an ensemble of accelerated ions with temperature $T$

$$ \xi = T\frac{\gamma k_\mathrm{B}f}{\sqrt{8qUmc^2}}, $$

where $\gamma$ is the relativistic time-dilation factor.

Parameters:
tarray_like

The temperature $T$ of the ions (K).

farray_like

The rest-frame transition frequency $f$ (arb. units).

uarray_like

The acceleration voltage $U$ (V).

qarray_like

The electric charge $q$ of the ions (e).

marray_like

The mass $m$ of the ions (u).

Returns:
xindarray

The asymmetry parameter $\xi$ (unit of f).
