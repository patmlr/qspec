qspec. t_sigma  (  sigma ,  m  )[source]

The temperature of the environment, given the standard deviation $\sigma_v$ of a normal distribution of particle velocities

$$ T = \frac{m\sigma_v^2}{k_\mathrm{B}}. $$
Parameters:
sigmaarray_like

The standard deviation $\sigma_v$ (m/s).

marray_like

The mass $m$ of the particle (u).

Returns:
Tndarray

The temperature $T$ of the environment (K).
